HYUNGRYUL HARRY BAIK / 2022 KAIX SUMMER SCHOOL IN MATHEMATICS

2022 KAIX Summer School in Mathematics

KAIST hosts a Summer School in Mathematics from July 25th to August 4th, 2022 (jointly organized by Harry Hyungryul Baik, Jaeyoung Byeon  and Cuong Nguyen at KAIST). 

Myoungjean Bae, Kyeongsik Nam, Junghwan Park, Ngoc Cuong Nguyen will give mini-courses in PDE, Probability theory, Topology, and Complex analysis. 

There will be some invited talks by speakers from outside of KAIST. 

All talks are at the building E6-1, Room ****.
